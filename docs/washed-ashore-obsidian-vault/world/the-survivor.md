# The Survivor

_The Survivor is the man the player plays: a tired, realistic adult washed up on the island. In first person you see his hands, forearms, watch, flannel and boots; his shadow shows the whole man._

## In the game
- **First person:** both hands and bare forearms, the watch on his RIGHT wrist, the open flannel hanging at both sides when looking straight down, trousers and boots. His head and hair are hidden.
- **Shadow:** the full figure in every pose and gait. View and shadow use the same skinned body, so the shadow can't be mirrored.
- **Clothes:** flannel over a T-shirt, patched cargo trousers, boots. No backpack at the start; a backpack is a later pickup (#95).

## Design intent
- He reads as an upright, tired, realistic man (director, S6). Compared with the low-poly Farmer, where the camera sat inside the hat, it is "a clear step up".
- The watch stays, on the right wrist (amend 7). Whether a watch in view should tell the time is parked for design.
- Made in Meshy by Noah; characters are all-in on Meshy ([[0009-survivor-is-the-player-body]]).
- Realistic proportions on a shared human skeleton that later bodies, including the women's base (#36), use ([[0010-realistic-human-skeleton-low-poly-humans-removed]]).

## Current state
- **Height:** about 1.85 m. He was modelled at 1.80 m and scaled up so his eyes meet the camera ([[0011-survivor-scaled-to-1-85-m]]).
- **Hands:** mitten fingers, weighted to the wrist. Noah accepted this ("That's fine", 2026-11-04); the gaps between fingers are intended.
- **Fabric:** no normal map, by Noah's call, so cloth reads flat up close. A follow-up, not a fail.
- **Known see-through:** small fold gaps in the trouser legs at steep look-down in deep bends, accepted under W-49-5 ([[0015-fold-gaps-accepted-w-49-5]]).
