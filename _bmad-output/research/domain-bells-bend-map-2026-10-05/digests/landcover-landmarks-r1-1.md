# Bells Bend: land cover, roads, structures and landmarks (digest r1-1)

Accessed 2026-10-05. Scope: Bells Bend peninsula (river-bounded, south of about 36.205 N) plus a NORTH strip (36.204 to 36.24 N, -86.96 to -86.885) that covers Scottsboro and TN-12.

**OSM method.** I used Overpass queries (overpass-api.de, 2026-10-05). The peninsula polygon is built from the Cumberland River centreline (OSM way 40806685) and closed along roughly TN-12. Zones are split by latitude: PEN-N is 36.175 N or more, PEN-C is 36.155 to 36.175 N, and PEN-S is below 36.155 N. The polygon is approximate, so features near the neck may sit just outside the true peninsula.
