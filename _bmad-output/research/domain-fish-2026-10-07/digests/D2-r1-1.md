## Reading for the lore (INFERRED)
- In 1987 (the collapse) neither species was in the Cumberland; the nearest established populations were in the Mississippi/Ohio main stems at best (Ridgway's "by the 1980s" is the earliest bound; TWRA puts the escapes in the late 1980s-early 1990s).
- With no dam operation from 1987, the locks at Barkley (head about 57 ft) and Cheatham are shut. TWRA and Vallazza both place upstream passage at the locks. Two dams without lockage stand between the Ohio and Bells Bend.
- Plausible: silver carp abundant below Barkley Dam and in the lower Ohio by 2012. Not plausible: natural spread past Barkley and Cheatham into the Cheatham pool. Recommendation for G2: OUT; common carp stands in (present since the 1800s, MDC).
