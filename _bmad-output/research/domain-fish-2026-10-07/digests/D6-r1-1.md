## Arithmetic (INFERRED)
- Unfished fish sit at the low end of the measured FID range: 1-3 m for a diver in clear water. A wading or swimming human is louder (vibration, splash) and fish in murk rely on the lateral line rather than sight, so the trigger is set a little higher than the unfished-diver value: wading 4 m, swimming 6 m, walking the bank lip 2.5 m (footfall over the shelf).
- Settle: no measured return time was found. Game value 45 s (30-90 s), chosen so a player standing still sees the shallows refill within a minute (supports F6).
