## Arithmetic (INFERRED)
- Real: Secchi 1.0 m implies k ~ 1.7 / m. Shader: mean k = 2.6 / m implies a Secchi ~ 0.65 m. The shader is about 1.5x murkier than the measured pool. Per N1 the shader wins; the measured 0.7-1.2 m stays as the reference.
- Shader transmittance straight down: T(0.3 m) = 0.46, T(0.6 m) = 0.21, T(0.8 m) = 0.12, T(1.0 m) = 0.07.
- From shore eye height 1.7 m looking at a point 3 m out: N.V ~ 0.49, so L = 2.0 d. T(0.3 m) = 0.20, T(0.6 m) = 0.04. At 5 m out (N.V ~ 0.32): T(0.3 m) = 0.09.
- Consequence: from the bank only fish in the top ~0.4 m within ~4 m of the bank can show a body or shadow. Everything else is seen only through surface signs. That matches N1 and N2.
- Post-collapse clarity: less urban and farm runoff would likely clear the water (raise Secchi). Not used: the shader is settled.
