## Pattern (INFERRED)
1. Games that can't afford, or don't want, an underwater view sell fish through **surface cues** (ripples, splashes, jumps), as RDR2 tells players to do. That is exactly N1.
2. Fish are **anchored** to spawn locations or zones and keep to a depth range (Valheim, SotF). Our equivalent: bands by WaterMaps shore distance and bed depth, plus structure anchors.
3. Populations are **local to the player** (RDR2 spawn areas, Far Cry 500 m bubble). Fish have the smallest bubble of any wildlife because murk hides them beyond a few metres.
4. Hard no's for us: glowing highlights (RDR2 Eagle Eye), visible open-water schools at the player's feet (SotF ocean), abstraction to nothing (TLD).
