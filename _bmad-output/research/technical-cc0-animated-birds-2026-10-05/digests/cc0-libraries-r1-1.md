## Leads

- SoltorchGames' $5 Forest and Highlands packs (7 birds, 197 and 137 clips) suit a wilderness setting if a small budget is acceptable. Their license text was not read.
- Check whether the SoltorchGames Robin includes a glide or takeoff pose under "Fly" and "Flutter", and test the FBX in Unity 6 (bone scale, root motion).
- OGA `/content/rooster-animated` and `/content/chicken-animated`: read each page for license, formats and clip names.
- Gobkit "Animal Pack Vol. 2" (10 more creatures) may contain a bird; its creature list was not read.
- SoltorchGames lists "LookToTheBirds" as the product line name. There may be a central page with more free samples.
