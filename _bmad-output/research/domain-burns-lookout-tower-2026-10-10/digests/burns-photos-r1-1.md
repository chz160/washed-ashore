## Leads

- **YouTube VpTGZRPrPN0** "Climbing a Montgomery bell state park fire tower", Grant Escue, 2:45, 2016-02-21. Storyboard frame time is about (sheet x 9 + index) x 2 s:
  - 0:00-0:18 (M0): stair flights from below, with site buildings in view.
  - 0:36-0:52 (M2): wooden landing close-ups.
  - About 0:58-1:10 (M3): top-level views and the grid dish. The video does not clearly enter the cab.
  - 1:12 onward (M4-M8): descent, with top-down views of the stair and landings.
  - Download the full video with yt-dlp for proper frames.
- Street View for the Hwy 47 gate and railroad underpass: try https://www.google.com/maps/@36.0644,-87.2828,17z and Street View on TN-47 near the HQ entrance. I did not verify that coverage exists.
- Google Maps place photos for "Tennessee Division of Forestry Highland Rim District" in Burns. These were not reachable by curl.
- NHLR "Next/visit report" pages may get more photos added later. I found no FFLA (firelookout.org) page for this tower; it returned 404.
