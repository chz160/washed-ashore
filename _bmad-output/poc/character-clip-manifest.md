## Limits named here

- Walk-speed strafe and walk-back clips do not exist (the pack has Run_Left/Run_Right/Run_Back only, about 3.0 m/s). Below that speed strafing and
  backing play the Run_* clips at a reduced rate; foot slide there is not covered by the 0.10 m check. Future issue 14 (td).
- Strafe and back clip speeds equal the forward node speeds in every family (Run 3.03 / Run_Back 3.03 / Run_Left 3.03 / Run_Right 3.03; Jog, Sprint and CrouchWalk identical in all four directions), so CharacterClipSet.LocomotionRate holds while strafing.
- Locomotion blend (td): Walk (stand forward) and CrouchWalk play alone from 0.7 to 1.4 x their on-body speed (rate edge exactly 0.70; crouch 1.25 to 2.5 m/s is rate 0.70 to 1.40) and blend toward Idle only below the 0.70 edge. Strafe and back play Run_* alone from 0.7 to 1.0 x their speed. Jog and Sprint play alone at 5 and 8 m/s.
- Foot plant: authored locomotion clips are keyed at 60 fps; the first and last swing sample of each step keeps moving back with the ground while already clear of the 3 cm plant band, so the in-band foot does not slide. The drift column is the largest horizontal distance between any two samples of a plant, sampled 4x between keys, body travel at the clip's own speed removed.
