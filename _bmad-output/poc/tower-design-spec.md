# Lookout tower: climb and interaction design

**Player problem.** The tower is the one place on the bend where the player can see all of it. The climb has to cost
something (time, height, exposure) and the cab has to pay it back, without one piece of UI telling them so.

**Loop it serves.** Explore, see the tower on the skyline, walk the track, climb, orient, pick the next place to go.

**Not in scope (FAIL if built):** fall damage, sound, sway, FOV pulse, vignette, crosshair, compass, bearing HUD, markers,
outlines, glows, icons, loot, notes, auto-opening or auto-closing doors, teleports or fades on the climb.

Two facts drive the whole design:

- **The capsule doesn't fit.** It is 0.80 m wide (0.90 with skin). The stairs are 0.61 m between stringers, the smallest

- **Walk speed is 6 to 10 times too fast.** See §2.4.

## 2. Climb (C1, C2, N9)

### 2.1 Requirements the traversal solution must meet

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | Holding **W only** (plus mouse turns on landings) takes the player from the ground to the cab floor. No jump, no other key |
| R2 | Base to cab at walk speed takes **60-120 s**; design centre 70-85 s |
| R3 | Releasing W stops the player within 0.05 m. They hold position on a flight indefinitely with no slide or creep (≤ 0.01 m over 10 s) |
| R4 | The player can turn freely at any point and walk back down. S while facing up the flight descends backward |
| R5 | Every landing needs a real player-made turn. Nothing rotates the camera for them |
| R6 | No teleport, fade, cutscene or input lock anywhere between ground and cab. Position is continuous: no frame moves the **feet** more than `speed x dt + 0.02 m`, where speed is the along-slope stair speed or `towerWalkSpeed` in force. | R9 | Rails and landing edges can't be crossed by walking, strafing, sprinting or jumping. The player can't enter the frame or a flight from the side |

### 2.2 Behaviour on the tower

| Input or situation | Flights | Landings | Cab |
|---|---|---|---|

Jump is off on the whole tower because the 1.2 m jump clears the 1.07 m landing rail, and the head-hit behaviour in §1 would
hang the player under the flight above. Sprint is off by default because N9 asks for a walk; it is a retune lever.

**Jump conditions (ruling/lookout-tower-n9-jump-capsule R1):** a jump press on the tower is silent (no message, prompt,
sound, animation or camera twitch); walk, strafe, turn, stop and look stay fully live; jump works again on the first
frame the player is off the first tread, with no delay.

### 2.3 Technique (ruled by t-td, tower-td-note.md §6)

**Flight lanes, real landings, and a small capsule inside the climb area.** Rejected: invisible 52° ramps with a
slope-limit override (the capsule still doesn't fit, climb time is emergent, C2 risk at joints) and one spline from base
to cab (no player-made turn on a landing, so it fails N9). The code is generic and lives in Gameplay (`StairLane`,
`ClimbArea`, `ClimbRules`, `ClimbTuning`); "climb area" below is the tower today and any later stair or ladder.

1. **Lane.** A segment from `foot` to `head` along the nosing line (within 0.02 m of it); its `width` is that flight's stringer spacing from the manifest. Lane ends are
   FBX empties from the build script (`LANE_Fnn_Foot`, `LANE_Fnn_Head`); scalars come from the parameter table. None by hand.
2. **On a lane.** `wish` = the usual yaw-relative input vector. `along = dot(wish, axisHorizontal)`. If `|along| < 0.2` the
   player doesn't move. Otherwise speed = `stairAscendSpeed` (up) or `stairDescendSpeed` (down), measured along the slope,
   scaled by `clamp01(|along| / 0.5)` so anything within 60° of the axis is full speed at full input. Reached with `stairAcceleration`. **(A2, pre-result correction; t-td ruled:)** every decision on a lane (joining, `laneJoinAlong`; passing a bend, `laneJunctionAlong`; the carry-first test) uses the direction of the yaw-relative input, and passes only when the input's projection on the lane also reaches `laneAlongDeadband`. Speed along a lane is set from the projection as written; with a stick this is not yet proportional to tilt as ground walking is: known difference, follow-up.
   Lateral offset is held within ± `laneLateralSlack`. The feet's height is the lane's height at that parameter. Look is free.
3. **Joining (A2; t-td's lane-end ruling, t-qa agreed).** The join mouth is the whole final level stretch at a free end.
   Per end and side, the band the player is held to is `min(W0 x (1 - s/length), reach(s))`, never widening toward the stair:
   `W0` = the smaller of half the lane's width x `laneMouthWidthFactor` and length x tan 40°; `reach(s)` = how far to that
   side a body at the measured wall reach (0.25 m sliding, 0.30 m head-on) can stand there. So **wherever a body can stand,
   W at the stair takes it** (coverage of the reachable cross-section is 100% and the build asserts it). At the lip the
   band is the reachable lip width, not zero; a flight's band eases between that and `laneLateralSlack` over
   `laneTaperLength`. Under an opening a body can drop through (the hatch) the flight's band is the slot's reachable
   width. The offset closes by at most tan 40° of forward travel, except where a drawn rail or wall bounds the reachable
   set (named in the manifest); it holds if the player stops, never grows, never snaps (R6). The wish must be within 60°
   of the lane's axis (`laneJoinAlong`). A player who drops into a lane from above joins when the feet reach its height.
   The lane just left can't be rejoined until the player has moved `laneRejoinDistance` or re-pressed the input; **A15 (t-td's ruling, revision 2, sha256 37ad3331…, on slot 31's finding; team-lead):** inside that distance the lane just left is also rejoined when the input lies within the junction lever (`laneJunctionAlong`) of straight back into it: turning straight back at the end of a flight takes the player back along it at once; steering at the next stair does not. No new value; nothing is retired. To be shown on the layout of record (RC-6'): at every hand-over point and each direction of travel, the heading to the next stair is more than the junction lever off straight back, by the margin the ruling states before any layout exists; a landing under it goes to t-director and t-td by name.
4. **Leaving, junctions and landings (A2; td-note §12a and the lane-end ruling; frozen by t-producer).** A lane is left only
   through a **free end**, at a position the controller can stand, clear at radius + skin and ≥ 0.30 m from any drop. Two
   coincident lane ends form a **junction** (exactly two, never two flights): the player is carried onto the other lane
   only with the wish at least `laneJunctionAlong` along it; **the carry test is evaluated before the on-lane rule**;
   otherwise they stay on the current lane under the ordinary on-lane rule, and input along neither holds them at the
   junction. A junction with no turn (within the build's bend guard of zero) is not a corner: it is passed by the ordinary on-lane rule in both directions, as if the two lanes were one, with speed continuous; the lever applies to bends only (t-td R4a). Nothing nudges, turns or carries them (ruling/lookout-tower-top-landing-and-step-off). Kinds, from the
   manifest: **flight** (on the nosing line); **run-out** (level, `laneRunOutLength`, the level end of a flight's own
   lane; "run-out lane" in §9 means any level stretch at a flight end, built-in or linked, marked per end by start, end
   and length); **exit** (lane 9, level, from flight 8's head `<0.70>` m to the cab floor west of the hatch). Behaviour:
   landings 1 and 2 straight on (built-in heads); **3 and 4 stop at both ends, pending the search (A13):** rung 4 of the ladder, linked run-outs, the same form as landing 6; rungs 1 to 3 (carried at one end or both) are struck by the feasibility print (`feas_l34.json`, sha256 056471c3…: every carried end NO) and are not searched; the one whole-tower search requires every clearance with at least 0.005 m in hand (team-lead's floor, fixed before any result, `tower-search-margin-floor.md`); a landing with no layout at rung 4 falls to rung 5, the joined path, and Noah is told first; unknown until the search prints, not promised; 5 and 7 linked level lanes that carry through (`<29-31>`° toward; landing 7 down `<27>`°
   away, a named exception); 6 a deliberate stop at a bend of `<45.0>`° (≥ lever + 10°); the cab a stop at `<104>`°.
   Layout checks read the tuning value: every junction is ≤ the lever's angle or ≥ it + 10° (no twitch stops); carried
   ≤ 40° toward the next flight and ≤ 30° away (t-director); a junction's turn is under 180° − 2 x the lever's angle (R4b). Level stretches move at `towerWalkSpeed`, changing speed
   at `stairAcceleration`; a sprint into flight 1 is cut to `towerWalkSpeed` on the join frame.
5. **A lane has no physics.** Feet position is a pure function of (lane, distance along it, lateral offset); static
   collision is ignored while on a lane (the standing capsule can't pass a real-size hatch). Moving blockers are handled
   by a gate: the top lane stops at `hatchStopHeadroom` below the hatch unless the hatch is fully open.
6. **Small capsule inside the climb area.** From the first lane's foot until the player leaves that foot again, the capsule radius is
   `towerCapsuleRadius` 0.25 m (0.60 m with skin; fits a 0.64 m landing and the cab lane). Height, eye and step stay. It
   returns to 0.4 only when a 0.4 capsule fits at the player's position (overlap check, **repeated every frame** until it
   passes), so it can't pop the player out of geometry. **The state heals itself:** Respawn, a `WorldBoundsClamp` snap, and
   being outside the climb area's bounds each clear lane and climb state in the same frame. Radius is the only capsule
   value ever written; slopeLimit, stepOffset, height, centre and skinWidth are never touched.
   Conditions (same ruling, R2): the change is imperceptible (no pop, push, snap or camera shift at the first tread or on
   leaving); the camera never shows through or inside any tower part (R8); the small capsule opens no route a person
   couldn't take (nothing between rail and stringer, through bracing or off a landing); nothing on the model is widened
   or thinned to suit it. **Gusset plates (A16; team-lead's wording; A13's sentence, with plates at 20 hanger nodes and a count of 84, is withdrawn):** plates are modelled at the leg joints as the standard sheet draws them (1/4 in, outline the artist's choice); none at the brace crossings, where the sheet shows fillers and the braces lapped; a bolted plate observed on the photographs at two strut nodes high on the tower is not modelled (64 plates, all at the leg joints). The 1/4 in fillers the standard sheet letters at the brace crossings are not modelled; the braces are drawn lapped. The ground at the first flight's foot is clear for 1.0 m all round (t-level, t-artist).
7. **Off-lane collision.** Each flight also carries a solid wedge and `WorldBounds` side walls 2.2 m high, so a player on
   the ground can't climb or jump onto a flight from its side or walk through it (R9). **PENDING, not design text (A13; team-lead):** the mouth gate, the proposed cure of C2-QA-1: a thin collider, its deck-side face on the mouth line, its thickness to the stair side, at 15 stair mouths (none at the head of flight 8; the hatch ruling stands; team-lead's word of 2026-10-13 on t-director's M3, superseding the 16 of t-td's proposal 6c8ea038…); a live body is stopped at any heading and passes only on the lane. Approved for proof on the rig; not yet proven. t-director accepts it only as a provisional named exception, on conditions M1 to M6 (`ruling/lookout-tower-mouth-gate-n9-amend2`); geometry by t-td's MG1 to MG5 (`tower-mouth-gate-join-ruling.md`, sha256 2e82e30f…); one case is open against t-director's M1: a reversal without a neutral input frame (td-note §137 D3). G6 gains "walk across a mouth at a slant outside the cone". It becomes design text when the rig is green and team-lead says so.

### 2.4 Climb time

Stair rise is 9" (0.2286 m) per step: the drawings give 9 steps for a 6'9" panel. At 52.4° (12 in 9¼) one step is 0.2887 m
of slope. **The tower is the 86'3" standard** (team-lead, 2026-10-10: cab floor 26.289 m above the footing tops, 8
flights). Flights top down: 9, 9, 12, 14, 17, 18, 18, 18 = **115 steps, 8 flights, 7 landings** (115 x 0.2286 = 26.289 m).
**A2:** flights 1-7 are 52.4° and the top flight 56.3° (team-lead, from T-1006-A); the stair is laid out to T-1004-A
with 5'-2" x 2'-6" platform landings; decks sit 2" above their steel levels (cab walking surface 26.3398 m); flight 8 runs
south along the east wall into the hatch. Built figures on the export of record: stair path `<m>`, level stretches `<m>`,
predicted climb `<s>` (pre-rebuild estimate: 33.19 m of stair, 74.7-77.7 s at 0.45 m/s).

**The stairs need their own speed.** At walk speed the climb is 7 s, a ninth of the minimum. As a multiplier on walk,
0.45 m/s is **x 0.09**; it is specified as an absolute speed so a later walk retune doesn't move the climb. It is 0.36 m/s
of vertical gain, about 1.6 steps a second, which matches the 2016 reference climb (about 60-70 s to the top).

Formula QA uses with the built lane lengths: `T = Σ lane length / stairAscendSpeed + Σ landing crossing / towerWalkSpeed`.
A person adds about 0.5-1 s of turning per landing, so Noah's climb should read 78-85 s.
`stairAscendSpeed` stays inside 60-120 s between **0.30 and 0.54 m/s**; that is its allowed range.
t-director accepted 0.45 as the starting value and expects "slower" as the likely retune: N9 aims at the upper half of
the band (90-120 s), which is 0.29-0.38 m/s here. 0.36 m/s gives 93-96 s.

## 3. Interaction system (I1, N11, N12)

### 3.1 Contract

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Input | One new code-created action `Interact`: `<Keyboard>/e`, `<Gamepad>/buttonSouth`. Same style as the existing actions; the unused `.inputactions` asset stays unused |
| Ray | From the camera position along camera forward, length `interactRange` **2.5 m** (hard cap 2.5), once per frame |
| Mask | `WorldLayers.SightMask` (no Water, no `WorldBounds`), triggers ignored. No new layer |
| Target | The **nearest hit only**. If that collider or a parent is an interactable, it is the target. Any other solid hit means no target: walls, floors, the hatch leaf and closed sashes occlude |
| Interactable | Gives: `Verb` and `Noun` for its current state, `CanInteract(player)`, `Interact(player)`. Its interact collider is its own solid collider (sash frame box, hatch leaf, firefinder body). A sash's box covers the whole sash, missing panes included |
| Can't interact | No prompt and E does nothing. No "locked" or greyed text |
| Moving | An interactable that is animating has no prompt and ignores E. No queueing, no reversing mid-swing |
| Feedback | The thing moves. Nothing else: no outline, glow, pulse, icon, sound or crosshair change |
| For later sync | Each interactable has a stable string id from the build script, an explicit state, and one apply method separate from input. Its animation is a pure function of (state, time since the change) |

### 3.2 The prompt line

- One uGUI Text in the built-in LegacyRuntime font with a Shadow component, built by script, centred, baseline at 30% of screen height from the
  bottom, cap height 2.4% of screen height (font size = cap height / `promptCapToEm`), off-white
  with a 1 px dark shadow. No panel, icon, glyph image, animation or pulse. Appears and disappears within 0.1 s.
- Format: `{key} – {Verb} {noun}` with an en dash and single spaces. Verb capitalised, noun lower case.
- `{key}` follows the device that last sent input: keyboard `E`; Xbox pad `A`; PlayStation pad `Cross`; any other pad `South`.
- Shown only while the ray has a target whose `CanInteract` is true, and for the firefinder's exit line (below).

| Target and state | Prompt (keyboard) |
|---|---|
| Hatch closed, clear | `E – Open hatch` |
| Hatch open, clear | `E – Close hatch` |
| Hatch moving, or the player is in its way (§4) | none |
| Hinged sash closed | `E – Open window` |
| Hinged sash open | `E – Close window` |
| Sash moving; fixed sash; any pane | none |
| Firefinder, not in use | `E – Use firefinder` |
| Firefinder in use, first `firefinderPromptSeconds` (4 s) | `E – Leave firefinder` |
| Firefinder in use after that; anything else in the world | none |

### 3.3 Sharing E and South

| Situation | E | Gamepad South |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt showing | Interact | **Interact. Jump is not fired for that press** |
| No prompt, off the tower | Nothing | Jump, as today |
| No prompt, on the tower | Nothing | Nothing (jump is off there) |
| Using the firefinder | Tap leaves; hold rotates (§5) | Tap leaves |

E has no other binding today, so nothing else changes. Later interactables implement the same contract; nothing in the
interactor knows about towers.

**Blocking rules (it can't trap, crush or launch):**

   launch them) and standing in the opening on the top flight when closing (it would crush them).
2. While the leaf moves, the top lane's gate is shut and a prebuilt `WorldBounds` box (switched on and off, never created
   at runtime) fills the swing volume, so nobody can walk

3. The door works from both sides: from the top flight below (aim up) and from the cab floor beside it. So the player can
