# Lookout tower restart: which rulings carry over

Game director's decision, 13 Oct 2026 (about 21:50Z), on Noah's delegation. The first attempt closed as a FAIL against the
spec (stair-landing blocker three times). The restart puts the script-generated tower into the game with ordinary colliders
and the normal player controller. Rule applied: a ruling carries over only if it is about the tower itself, about the
brief, or is an answer Noah gave. Also recorded in the studio memory store as `studio:decisions`
`decision/lookout-tower-restart-carry-over`. Text below is the director's, unedited.

## 1. Carries over

### (a) The brief and Noah's answers
- SPEC: pass criteria T1-T4, P1-P4, C1-C3, I1-I5 and the automatic-FAIL list bind unchanged (two conflicts with later answers are under 3).
- SPEC: built by script only; photos are reference, never shipped or traced; real size 1:1; Central Ridge Summit through the terrain pipeline's projection.
- SPEC: out of scope stays out: fall damage, sound, keeper's cabin, multiplayer doors.
- NOAH: shutters and roof per the drawing: four shutters, open 72 degrees, static.
- NOAH: do not thin the fog; open water counts for the view; far limit 1.15 km accepted.
- NOAH: Windows only unless he asks for web; "keep the definition" of an assist.

### (b) What the tower is and how it looks
- SPEC + height decision (t-artist, read): 86'3" to the cab floor, 8 flights, 7 landings, 115 risers, 9 in riser, stair 2'0" wide.
- SPEC: 2012 condition: weathered white paint, rust streaks, about 30 percent of cab panes missing, grey rotting plank landings still passable, bent mast and Yagi, no working electronics.
- DIRECTOR (greenlight notes, as QA's AF8 row lists them): no in-world text naming Burns, Bell, Montgomery Bell, Dickson or NHLR; no grid dish; one bent mast and one bent Yagi; no HQ, shed, car, power line or cabin; no emissive material; frame plumb with no member missing.
- DIRECTOR: on a departure from the sheets "the drawing wins"; every departure is named to Noah, none hidden to pass a check.
- DIRECTOR: today's generator state stands as the starting model (64 corner plates, flight mid rails off, top flight 12 in 8, 40 mm landing guard post, straw-tone firefinder scale).

### (c) What the player may and may not experience
- SPEC: W only, no jump needed, about 60-120 s base to cab; can stop, turn, walk back down.
- SPEC: cannot fall off, clip through members, stick on a landing, end inside the frame, or be launched by a jump.
- SPEC: HUD is one prompt line, shown only while aiming at an interactable; firefinder bearing is in the world, not HUD.
- DIRECTOR: no teleport, fade, cutscene or input lock between ground and cab.
- DIRECTOR: every landing needs a turn the player makes; nothing rotates the camera for them.
- DIRECTOR: releasing W stops the player; they hold on a flight with no slide or creep.
- DIRECTOR: no sway, bob, FOV change, vignette or roll; no per-tread jolting of the eye.
- DIRECTOR: looking down through the plank gaps is wanted; vertigo comes from geometry only.
- DIRECTOR: no outline, glow or icon on interactables; nothing opens or closes by itself.
- DIRECTOR: the camera never shows the inside of a tower solid (the principle; the figure is under 4).
